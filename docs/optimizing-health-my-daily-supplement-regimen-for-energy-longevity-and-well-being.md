Modern life is chaotic. We juggle careers, relationships, fitness, and personal growth, all while trying to stay healthy and energetic. And yet, without our health, none of those pursuits mean much. Over time, I’ve refined a supplement regimen designed to support my physical and mental health, enhance longevity, and ensure that I’m functioning at my best every day.

My approach is rooted in a few key principles: **efficiency, effectiveness, and simplicity**. Every supplement in my routine has a role—whether it's boosting supporting cellular energy, cognitive function, or metabolic health. I also aim to time my supplements strategically throughout the day to maximize absorption and effectiveness. Below, I’ll walk you through my current regimen and the rationale behind each supplement.

> This post is **not** sponsored. I'm sharing what I take in hopes that it might help others building their own regimens out there. I have no financial interest in recommending these particular supplement brands, but do so because they're who I use and trust from years of experience with them.

Seneca reminds us, *"Life is long if you know how to use it."* We don’t control how many years we get, but we do control how well we function in those years.

My regimen is built around science-backed supplements that address key areas of health: hydration, mitochondrial function, metabolism, cognitive support, gut health, and recovery. Obviously, it’s not about taking as many supplements as possible—it’s about taking the right ones at the right times to optimize performance and longevity.

Of course, supplements are just one piece of the health puzzle. I also prioritize nutrition, exercise, sleep, and mindfulness to maintain a balanced lifestyle. But, these supplements have played a critical role in helping me feel my best, stay focused, and support my long-term health.

Supplements are never a shortcut, and they won’t compensate for poor diet, lack of exercise, or bad sleep. But when used strategically, they can help optimize performance, clarity, and longevity.

If you're building your own regimen, take the Stoic approach: Question everything. Test what works. Strip away what doesn’t. Do your research, consult with a healthcare provider, and tailor your regimen to your specific needs and goals. Health isn’t a destination—it’s a discipline, and an ongoing process of refinement and optimization.
